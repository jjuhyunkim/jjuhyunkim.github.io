# Verkko-Fillet for Assembly Curation

## Overview

Verkko-Fillet is a practical curation workflow for improving complete genome assemblies after the initial assembly stage. The project addresses graph complexity, unresolved edges, and error-prone repetitive regions.

## My Role

- Designed the curation strategy and implementation flow.
- Developed and tested methods on human and non-human assemblies.
- Connected assembly quality checks with downstream interpretation.

## Methods and Data

- Long-read assembly graph inspection and refinement.
- Post-assembly processing and quality review.
- Applied to complete assemblies including giraffe and human-derived samples.

## Current Progress

- Pipeline and troubleshooting guidance are in active use.
- Ongoing improvements target difficult repeat structures and reproducible curation steps.

## Paper 
> Kim, Juhyun, et al. "Finishing a complete giraffe genome from telomere to telomere with Verkko-Fillet." BioRxiv (2025).
